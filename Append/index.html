Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does your work proposal and approval process function before starting a cleaning job?

We prioritize transparency and collaboration. Upon receiving a request, we provide a detailed work proposal outlining the scope of the cleaning services, proposed schedules, and cost estimates. We await client approval before commencing any work to ensure alignment with expectations and requirements.

2. Do you offer contract-based cleaning services, and what are the advantages of entering into a contract with your company?

Yes, we offer contract-based cleaning services. Entering into a contract provides several benefits, including discounted rates, regular and scheduled cleaning services, priority scheduling, and a dedicated point of contact for efficient communication and issue resolution.

3. Can you accommodate special requests or customize cleaning services for specific business needs?

>Absolutely! We understand that each business has unique requirements. Our team is open to accommodating special requests and can customize our cleaning services to address specific needs. We encourage clients to discuss their preferences during the initial consultation.

4. How do I get started with your cleaning services for my business?

Initiating our cleaning services is a straightforward process. Simply contact us through our email or webside to schedule a consultation. During the consultation, we'll discuss your cleaning needs, provide a tailored proposal, and upon your approval, we'll work together to establish a cleaning schedule that suits your business operations.
